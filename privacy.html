KQPT / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how KQPT: King Queen Police Thief (the “app”) and this marketing website handle information. For privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests, contact the KQPT developer at visualmagix10@gmail.com.

App informationGoogle servicesStorage & deletionWebsite

1. Information used by the app

  • Local profile: your chosen nickname and avatar identify you in games and scorecards.
  • Gameplay and preferences: settings, saved groups and rosters, scores, statistics, XP, ranks and progression are stored on your device to support play and future sessions.
  • Local multiplayer: devices on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot exchange room and player information, scores and gameplay updates. The host manages the session, including roles, clues and tasks, in memory. Local match traffic is not sent to a KQPT central game server. Other participants can see your nickname and shared match information.
  • Nearby discovery: lobby name, player count, availability and connection metadata are announced on the local network. Private roles and room PINs are not included in discovery announcements.
  • Connection information: host address, port and room identifiers support joining and reconnecting. Room PINs, verified host fingerprints and reconnect credentials are stored using platform secure storage where available.

2. Permissions and connection security

Camera access is used to scan a host’s invite QR code. The scanner processes camera frames on the device; the app does not save or upload camera images or video. You can deny or revoke camera access in device settings and use the available manual joining flow.

Network and nearby Wi-Fi permissions support local room discovery and multiplayer. The Android nearby Wi-Fi permission is declared as not used for location. The app does not request GPS location for gameplay.

Local gameplay connections use TLS 1.3. Before first joining, scan the host’s QR code or compare the displayed Security Code with the host to verify the connection. The room PIN controls membership; it is not the encryption key. Share room invitations and PINs only with your play group. Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

3. Google services and other network requests

Google Play Games: the app attempts Play Games sign-in at startup and may use an existing Google sign-in session or show Google’s sign-in interface. Offline/local play remains available without a successful sign-in. When signed in, KQPT can submit your cumulative XP to a Google-hosted leaderboard. The integration can access the Play Games player display name. Google handles the associated account, service and leaderboard information under its own policies. KQPT does not ask for your Google password.

Google Play reviews: the app may offer an in-app rating prompt or open its store listing. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play.

Fonts: the app uses the Google Fonts library, which may fetch and cache fonts when they are not available locally. Such requests disclose connection information, including your IP address, to the font provider.

Google may process account, device and service information when you use its services. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Local app-data deletion does not delete Google-managed information.

4. Advertising, analytics and sharing

KQPT does not include third-party advertising or an app analytics/tracking SDK in the reviewed implementation. We do not sell or rent personal information or use it for cross-app behavioral advertising. Information is exchanged with your local play group for multiplayer and with the services described above when used. These service requests are distinct from local match traffic.

5. Retention, deletion and your controls

  • Local preferences, saved groups and progression remain on your device until changed or deleted using the relevant controls or device app-data clearing. Session gameplay is held in host memory; locally saved statistics and progression can persist after a match.
  • Edit your profile in Settings and manage or delete saved groups in Local Play.
  • “RESET ALL SETTINGS” resets preferences and saved connection details. It does not delete saved groups, statistics or progression.
  • Leave the lobby to clear saved connection credentials. Closing the host room invalidates its reconnect tokens but does not remotely erase another device’s storage.
  • Use Android’s system app settings to clear KQPT’s app storage and remove local app data. Platform secure-storage retention after uninstall can vary, so leave the lobby before uninstalling.
  • Manage Google Play Games information through Google’s account and Play Games controls. Removing KQPT’s local data does not remove leaderboard records held by Google.
  • For assistance or a privacy request, email visualmagix10@gmail.com. We cannot remotely access or erase data stored only on your device or another player’s device.

6. Marketing website and external links

This website has no account registration, gameplay sign-in, forms, advertising scripts or analytics scripts. Its own code does not set cookies or write to browser storage. Images are hosted with the website.

When the website is hosted, its hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser/request information and access logs to deliver and secure it, under the provider’s policies and retention practices. The website loads Google Fonts, which sends your browser’s font requests and connection information to Google.

Google Play and YouTube are external links, not embedded players. Following them opens those services, where their own policies apply. If you contact us by email, we receive your address and the information you provide, use it to respond, and retain correspondence as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. Avoid including passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Children and family play

KQPT is designed for family and social play. Use a nickname rather than a full real name, and avoid entering sensitive information. The app does not include behavioral tracking or targeted advertising. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of multiplayer, Google services and external links; those services have their own age and account requirements.

8. Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Send privacy questions, concerns or requests to visualmagix10@gmail.com.
